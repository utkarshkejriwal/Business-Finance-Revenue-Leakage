# Schedule A — Standard Rate Card and Charging Rules

Applies to every Merchant Service Agreement that references Schedule A, unless the agreement states otherwise. Amounts in INR, exclusive of GST.

## A.1 Forward freight (per shipment)

| Zone | First 0.5 kg | Each additional 0.5 kg |
|---|---|---|
| A | 32 | 14 |
| B | 38 | 18 |
| C | 45 | 24 |
| D | 52 | 28 |
| E | 70 | 38 |

## A.2 Zone determination

Zones are determined from the merchant's pickup city (origin) and the delivery city (destination), applying the first rule that matches, in this order:

1. Zone A: origin and destination are the same city.
2. Zone E: the destination is in a Special Region state (Assam, Meghalaya, Jammu and Kashmir).
3. Zone B: origin and destination are in the same state.
4. Zone C: origin and destination are both Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad).
5. Zone D: all other shipments.

## A.3 Chargeable weight

Chargeable weight is the higher of the dead weight and the volumetric weight. Volumetric weight (kg) = Length × Width × Height (cm) ÷ 5000, unless the Merchant Service Agreement specifies a different divisor. Chargeable weight is rounded up to the next 0.5 kg slab. The minimum chargeable weight is 0.5 kg unless the agreement specifies otherwise.

## A.4 Cash-on-delivery (COD) fee

Unless the agreement specifies otherwise, the COD fee for each COD shipment is the higher of INR 30 or 1.75% of the COD value. No COD fee applies to prepaid shipments.

## A.5 Discounts

Where an agreement grants a freight discount, it is applied as a percentage of the Schedule A forward freight (A.1) after the chargeable weight and zone are determined. Discounts never apply to the COD fee.

# Merchant Service Agreement — Megh Linen (M01)

This agreement is between Shadowfax Technologies Ltd. ("Shadowfax") and Megh Linen ("the Merchant"), effective 1 April 2026, for forward delivery of the Merchant's shipments within India. Pickups are made from the Merchant's warehouse in Delhi.

M01-§1.1 Shadowfax will pick up, transport and deliver the Merchant's shipments to end customers, and will collect cash on delivery where the shipment is booked as COD.

M01-§1.2 The Merchant will declare the weight and dimensions of each shipment at booking. Shadowfax may re-measure any shipment at its hubs, and the re-measured values are final for billing.

## M01-§2 Freight charges

M01-§2.1 Forward freight is charged at the Schedule A rate card. The Merchant receives a discount of 8% on Schedule A forward freight.

M01-§2.2 Chargeable weight is calculated as set out in Schedule A, section A.3.

## M01-§3 Cash on delivery

M01-§3.1 The COD fee follows Schedule A, section A.4.

M01-§3.2 COD amounts collected are remitted to the Merchant's nominated bank account twice weekly, net of any undisputed invoices that are overdue.

## M01-§4 Invoicing and payment

M01-§4.1 Shadowfax will invoice the Merchant monthly, on or before the 5th of the following month, with a shipment-level annexure.

M01-§4.2 Invoices are payable within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. Overdue amounts carry interest at 1.5% per month.

## M01-§5 Weight and billing disputes

M01-§5.1 The Merchant may dispute the chargeable weight or charges on any shipment within ten (10) days of the invoice date, by raising a ticket with supporting images showing the shipment on a calibrated scale.

M01-§5.2 Disputes not raised within this window are deemed accepted. Accepted disputes are settled through a credit note on the next invoice.

## M01-§6 Loss and damage

M01-§6.1 Shadowfax's liability for a lost or damaged shipment is limited to the lower of the declared invoice value of the shipment or INR 2,500 per shipment.

M01-§6.2 Claims must be raised within thirty (30) days of the shipment's booking date.

## M01-§7 Term and termination

M01-§7.1 This agreement runs for twelve (12) months and renews automatically for further twelve-month terms.

M01-§7.2 Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience by giving sixty (60) days' written notice.

## M01-§8 General

M01-§8.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of India, with courts in Bengaluru having exclusive jurisdiction.
